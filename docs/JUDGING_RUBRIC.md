# Judging Rubric

Use this to prepare the final demo.

## 1. Problem Clarity

- The team names a real community or township economy problem.
- The target user is specific.
- The pain is easy to understand.

## 2. Prototype

- The app runs locally.
- The main flow works.
- The role switcher shows 2-3 perspectives.
- Judges can understand the demo without logging in.

## 3. Business Case

- The team explains who adopts or pays.
- The path beyond event day is realistic.
- The idea has a practical first pilot.

## 4. Impact

- The solution connects to Soweto or Gauteng community needs.
- The team explains what would be measured after the event.

## 5. Build Discipline

- The team used Codex with clear prompts.
- The harness is visible: seed data, tests, scripts and checklist.
- `npm run check` passes or the team can clearly explain the remaining failure.
