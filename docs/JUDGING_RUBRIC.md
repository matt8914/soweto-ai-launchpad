# Judging Rubric

Use this to prepare the final demo.

## 1. Problem Clarity

- The team names a real community or township economy problem.
- The target user is specific.
- The pain is easy to understand.

## 2. Prototype

- The prototype runs locally or the clickable flow is easy to inspect.
- The main flow works.
- The team can show the relevant user perspectives.
- Judges can understand the demo without complex setup.

## 3. Business Case

- The team explains who adopts or pays.
- The path beyond event day is realistic.
- The idea has a practical first pilot.

## 4. Impact

- The solution connects to Soweto or Gauteng community needs.
- The team explains what would be measured after the event.

## 5. Build Discipline

- The team used Codex with clear prompts.
- The harness is visible: `AGENTS.md`, project brief, acceptance criteria, checks and demo script.
- The team can show what is green and explain any remaining red item.
