# Responsible Innovation

Build useful demos without putting real people at risk.

## Rules

- Use fictional names and demo data.
- Do not collect real ID numbers, addresses, medical details or GBV reports.
- Do not promise jobs, funding, medical help or emergency response unless the real partner exists.
- For safety, health and GBV ideas, show referral information and escalation paths as prototype content only.
- Be honest in the pitch about what is built today and what still needs validation.

## AI Use

Codex can help you build faster, but the team is responsible for truth, safety and taste.

If Codex invents statistics, partners, testimonials or prizes, remove them.
